Every senior engineer deserves a team.

TARS exists so the people who know the most stop spending their days searching, repeating and re-explaining, and start spending them on the problems only they can solve.

Abdallah Issa, founder

Abdallah works in embedded software, close to the test bench, the debugger and the ticket queue. That is where the idea for TARS came from: the answer to most tickets already exists somewhere in the company, in a spec, an old test, a tool manual or a colleague's head. Finding it is the slow part.

He started TARS to build the teammate he wanted: one that has read everything, works on the real tools, shows its evidence and never sends company data outside the building.

Abdallah on LinkedIn

What we believe

Your data stays yours

TARS runs on your machines. Privacy is not a setting you have to remember to turn on.

Show the evidence

An answer you cannot check is a guess. Every TARS conclusion links to what it is based on.

People decide

TARS proposes and does the legwork. Anything that changes a system or speaks for you waits for a person.

Easy beats clever

Adding knowledge, teaching a tool or asking a question should take minutes, not a project.
