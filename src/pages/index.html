From folder to answer in four moves.

  1. Connect

    Point TARS at your repositories, documents, ticket system and tools. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  2. Learn

    It reads everything locally and keeps the Knowledge Cave up to date as files change.

  3. Ask or investigate

    Ask a question, or hand over a ticket and watch every step live.

  4. Approve

    You approve anything that changes something. Then TARS delivers the result.

You stay in charge. Always.

TARS works like a careful colleague: it explains what it wants to do, waits for your yes on anything that matters, and stops the moment you say so.

  • Wind down lets the current step finish, then summarizes what was found.
  • Approvals for every action that changes something, and for every ticket comment.
  • Evidence behind every conclusion, so you can check the work in seconds.
